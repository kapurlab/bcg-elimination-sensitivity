# English translations of the founding BCG cattle papers

Complete English translations of three papers from the Annales de
l'Institut Pasteur, translated and formatted by Vivek Kapur (Penn State)
from the Gallica digitisations of the Institut Pasteur copies. Original page
numbers are marked in the text so that passages can be cited to the French
original.

| File | Paper |
| --- | --- |
| `Calmette_Guerin_1920_AnnInstPasteur_34_553-560_translation.pdf` | Calmette A, Guérin C. Nouvelles recherches expérimentales sur la vaccination des bovidés contre la tuberculose. Ann Inst Pasteur 1920;34(9):553-560. Thirty-four months of infecting cohabitation; resistance from day 30, not exceeding 18 months after a single dose, maintained by annual revaccination. |
| `Calmette_Guerin_1924_AnnInstPasteur_38_371-398_translation.pdf` | Calmette A, Guérin C. Vaccination des bovidés contre la tuberculose et méthode nouvelle de prophylaxie de la tuberculose bovine. Ann Inst Pasteur 1924;38(5):371-398. The subcutaneous method, and the design of the field trials: newborns within fifteen days, revaccinated each year, a five-year target. |
| `Guerin_Richart_Boissiere_1927_AnnInstPasteur_41_233-253_translation.pdf` | Guérin C, Richart A, Boissière M. Essai de prophylaxie de la tuberculose bovine par le BCG dans une exploitation rurale infectée. Ann Inst Pasteur 1927;41(3):233-253. The Gruville holding, 1921 to 1927: replacement of the infected herd by vaccinated and revaccinated animals. |
